# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 ｜ [正體中文](privacy)

## Scope

This policy applies to the iOS app SHILAI－Zi Wei (時來－紫微). Other apps in the SHILAI family have their own privacy policies.

## Data storage

Everything you enter in this app (chart name, birth date, birth time, chart gender, region) and everything it calculates is stored only on your own device, in UserDefaults. This app **does not upload or sync that data to any external server**.

Free-tier usage counters are stored in the device Keychain. They contain counts and dates only, never your birth details.

Please note: data stored on your device may be included in Apple device backups such as iCloud Backup. That backup behaviour is controlled by your Apple ID and iCloud settings; it is not something this app initiates. If you do not want this app's data backed up, you can turn it off in Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage App Backups on your iPhone.

## Third-party analytics

This app uses no third-party analytics SDK and no advertising tracking, and it never presents an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) request.

## Subscriptions

Subscription payments are handled by the Apple App Store. This app does not collect, see, or store your payment information. When it checks your subscription status it communicates only with Apple, and it never sends your chart or birth details.

## Deleting your data

This app does not currently have an in-app "erase everything" button. To delete all local data, remove the app from your device; the charts and calculated results stored on the device are deleted with it. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, please also follow the backup instructions above.

## What this app does not do

To avoid any misunderstanding: this app has no widget, no push or local notifications, and no user accounts. It does not collect contacts, location, or health data.
