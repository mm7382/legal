Nothing in this app **is legal, financial, medical, relationship, or any other professional advice**, and nothing in it is a prediction of, or a guarantee about, any particular event or outcome.

## About the interpretation rules

The interpretation rules in this app are still being researched and checked against sources, and some of them have not completed source verification. Every piece of text on screen states where it comes from and whether it has been verified. Rules that have not passed source verification are never used to mark anything as lucky, unlucky, better, or worse. Please do not rely on this app for important decisions.

## Accuracy of the chart

The chart depends entirely on the birth details you enter. If you choose "not known" for the birth time, no Zi Wei chart can be generated. This app does not guarantee that a chart matches any particular school or printed text.
