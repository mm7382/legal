## Frequently asked questions

SHILAI－Zi Wei generates a Zi Wei Dou Shu (紫微斗數) chart from your birth details, and shows the Heavenly Stem and Earthly Branch (干支) for today and for the next seven days, with neutral reference notes. Every note states its source and verification status. This app does not include Bazi, the farmer's almanac, or "recommended hours" features.

What you see is the chart itself plus text whose source is stated. The interpretation rules are still being researched and checked, so **nothing here is a guarantee or a prediction**. Rules that have not passed source verification are never used to mark one day as better or worse than another.
